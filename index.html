What is this mod?
Image of Soul is a free singleplayer action RPG Source Engine total-conversion mod focusing on high speed hack and slash combat. It is a fan project inspired by the anime MORPG SoulWorker. Image of Soul and its developer is not affiliated with VALOFE or LIONGAMES.

Features:

Hack and slash combat
Slice through cultists and monsters in satisfying actions.
Free roaming & Stage unlocks
Travel freely between levels after defeating the area boss. Grab hidden items and resources.

Inventory and drops
Use Items, equip Soulgears and weapons dropped by monsters or placed scattered around the island. Fight with new abilities earned from area vendors.

Crafting
Build effective Soulgears and weapons from your battle resources.

Background
You’re an agent sent to a research island investigating the recent disappearance of a highly skilled scientist who is developing an important instrument that could win the war for your organization. You took on the case to discover the truth behind a recent incident that happened in the city and your source of superhuman power.

About this mod

Image of Soul is a fan-made free Source Engine modification that replicates the experience of high speed hack-and-slash gameplay from the Action MORPG game SoulWorker


It is a singleplayer game and does not feature online connectivity.


The project is not affiliated with VALOFE and LIONGAMES.
